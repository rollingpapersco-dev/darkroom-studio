The show
Black room. The artist, lit.

Darkroom is a stripped-back music performance series with a signature black-and-white visual style. Each artist performs in an isolated black studio space, with controlled lighting drawing attention to their face, gestures, and silhouette while the background disappears into darkness.

The format alternates intimate close-ups with medium and full-body shots, using generous negative space and occasional layered images of the performer to create depth and movement. Bold typography introduces the artist and track, completing a consistent visual identity across episodes.

The artist remains the focus: their presence and expression give each performance its individuality within the same recognisable setting.
