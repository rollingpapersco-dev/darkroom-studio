Research · what the platforms reward in 2026
Held attention. Shared by DM.
INSTAGRAM

The top signals are watch time and sends per reach. A DM share weighs roughly 3–5× a like when Instagram decides whether to show a post to non-followers.

Trial Reels need 1,000 followers, so we can't use them yet. Accounts that post 10+ reposts in 30 days lose recommendations, so we post originals only.

YOUTUBE

Every Short can carry a Related video link to the full episode. Expect a trickle, not a flood: most Shorts viewers stay in the Shorts feed.

A Short and its full episode should share one playlist, with the link pinned. Hype opens once we join the Partner Program at 500+ subs.

TIKTOK

TikTok works like a search engine. It reads the caption, on-screen text and spoken words, so every clip names the artist, the song and "live in Lagos".

The first seconds decide whether a clip gets shown. Open on the vocal peak, not the intro.
