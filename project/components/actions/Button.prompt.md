Pill-shaped button in Darkroom's monochrome style; use `primary` for the one main action per section, `ghost` beside it.

```jsx
<Button variant="primary" href="/episodes.html">Stream Episodes</Button>
<Button variant="ghost" href="/pricing.html">View Pricing</Button>
```

- `nav` — small white "Apply Now" in the navbar
- `tier` / `tier-featured` — full-width CTAs at the bottom of pricing cards
- `outline` — "Watch" with ▶ glyph, over imagery
- `dock` / `dock-back` — multi-step form footer (Continue / Back)
